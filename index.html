Who we are

Building stronger futures through sport

Fecund Performance is a community-centered sports initiative dedicated to helping young people stay active, positive, and focused. We aim to reduce the influence of drugs, peer pressure, and inactivity by creating meaningful opportunities in sport, teamwork, and personal growth.

Our story

Founded through passion and purpose

Fecund Performance was founded by Jabulani Sibanda, a father who developed a love for basketball during his youth and has remained deeply committed to helping young people live healthier, more purposeful lives.

Together with Coach Jackson, Jabulani coaches and mentors children at Zwide Kings and Queens Primary School, giving them a safe and positive environment to develop through sport.

The mission is simple: empower the community, keep youth active, and offer positive alternatives to drugs and negative peer pressure.
